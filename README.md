The whole internet, in one word:

**tabsoup**

Forty-seven tabs open, three of them playing music you can't find, one
of them a recipe you opened in 2023, and you still won't close any of
them — because any one of them might be important someday. That is the
internet: not a library, not a town square, just a pot of tabs simmering
forever.

## How it works

`main.py` is stdlib-only. It writes the single entry word directly to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — no third-party imports, no network,
no reading of any other file. Run it with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
